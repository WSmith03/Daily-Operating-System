ADHD Protocols

Pick the one that matches the state. Follow it top to bottom. Don't improvise.

🧠 Task paralysis reset

  1. STOP. Breathe: 4 counts in, 4 out. Repeat ×3.
  2. Pick ONE task only. Close everything else.
  3. Set a 10 min timer. Just start — don't try to finish.
  4. Reward: 5 min walk immediately after the block.
  5. Repeat. Don't plan. Just start.

⚡ Low motivation

  1. Minimum Viable Day: 3 tasks, not 10.
  2. Do the easiest win first — build momentum from nothing.
  3. Change environment: café, different room, outside.
  4. Allowed: music, binaural beats, lo-fi, white noise.
  5. No self-criticism — log the day and move on.

🔄 Bad day reset

  1. Acknowledge: bad days are data, not failure.
  2. Emergency walk: 20 min outside, phone face down.
  3. Single task: complete one small, visible thing.
  4. Contact: message one person who grounds you.
  5. Sleep early and start fresh tomorrow.

🔀 Transition ritual

  1. End current task: write exactly where you left off.
  2. 2 min physical reset: stand, stretch, drink water.
  3. Review: what is the NEXT block?
  4. Set the timer for the next block before sitting down.
  5. Sit, breathe once, begin.

🎯 Focus entry ritual

  1. Clear your desk and screen completely.
  2. One tab, one task, one window — close everything else.
  3. Headphones on (signal: focus mode activated).
  4. Write the task name at the top of your page.
  5. Set a Pomodoro timer. Go.

🚨 Emergency reset

  1. STOP everything immediately — no half-measures.
  2. Go outside — even 5 minutes counts.
  3. Cold water on face and wrists.
  4. Write: what am I actually feeling right now?
  5. Choose: rest OR minimum viable work. No in-between.

The contract

Rules & Standards

Non-negotiables at the top. Everything else flexes around them.

🔴 Non-negotiables

  • Train minimum 4× per week
  • 8 hrs sleep — hard floor, not a target
  • 3L water daily
  • No phone first 30 min of the day
  • Log updated every single day
  • Protein target hit daily
  • At least 1 deep work block daily

🟡 Minimum standards

  • Even on bad days: drink water + move body
  • 1 task completed = successful day minimum
  • Bed before midnight every weekday
  • Shutdown ritual every single evening
  • Supplements every morning without exception

🟢 Burnout prevention

  • 1 full rest day per week — no guilt, no exceptions
  • No work after 17:00 at least 2 days per week
  • Monthly: 1 full day tech-free
  • Weekly: something with nothing to do with work
  • If exhausted: choose sleep over training, always

🔵 Travel protocol

  • Pack supplements for every trip — non-negotiable
  • Find gym or pool within 24 hrs of arrival
  • Maintain sleep schedule ±1 hr
  • Water bottle always in bag
  • The system travels with you — it's on your phone
  • No junk food on travel days — plan ahead
